# Contenido y crecimiento

Playbook para operar Instagram como canal de confianza, deseo y conversion hacia `bagelito.pe`.

## Objetivo

- Hacer que el perfil explique Bagelito en menos de 5 segundos.
- Convertir visitas de Instagram en reservas o waitlist.
- Usar fotos, videos y testimonios reales del batch.
- Crear urgencia natural alrededor de apertura, cierre, produccion y delivery.

- No poner link directo a WhatsApp en bio si se quiere medir conversion web.
- Usar WhatsApp directo solo en stories urgentes o DMs.
- Cambiar bio y link el mismo dia que cambia el status del batch.

## Highlights

Orden recomendado:

1. `Next batch`
2. `Menu`
3. `How it works`
4. `Delivery`
5. `Reviews`
6. `Behind`
7. `FAQ`

Contenido de cada highlight:

- `Next batch`: fecha de apertura, fecha de cierre, fecha de delivery, CTA a link de bio.
- `Menu`: packs, sabores, precios, mixed vs single flavor.
- `How it works`: reserva, pago Yape/Plin, confirmacion, horneado, delivery.
- `Delivery`: distritos, costo incluido en checkout, ventana de entrega, recepcion/porteria.
- `Reviews`: screenshots de feedback, fotos de clientes con permiso, frases cortas.
- `Behind`: masa, toppings, horno, empaque, checklist de batch.
- `FAQ`: cuanto duran, como guardar/congelar, como pedir, que pasa si se agota.

Estilo visual:

- Covers simples con fondo crema/blanco y texto navy.
- Usar 1 color por highlight, consistente con la web: pink, orange, mint, purple.
- Evitar covers genericos de stock. Mejor close-ups reales de bagels.

Shot list minimo antes de abrir el proximo batch:

- Foto vertical de 12 mixed completo.
- Foto vertical de 6 mixed completo.

- Foto vertical de 6 single flavor.

Reglas de captura:

- Luz natural, cerca de ventana.
- Fondo claro y limpio.
- Tomar vertical 4:5 para feed y 9:16 para stories/reels.
- Evitar filtros fuertes; que el producto se vea real.
- Sacar 3 variaciones por toma: cerca, medio, detalle.
- Guardar los mejores archivos en una carpeta por batch: `Batch YYYY-MM`.

## Testimonios reales

Pedir feedback despues de marcar `delivered` y enviar `Msg feedback`.

Mensaje corto para pedir testimonio:

```text
Hi {name}! Hope you enjoyed your Bagelito pack.
Could you send us a quick sentence about what you liked most?
If you are ok with it, we may share it on Instagram without showing private info.
```

Mensaje para pedir foto:

```text
If you took a photo of the bagels, send it over please.
We would love to share it in our stories and tag you only if you want.
```

Reglas:

- No publicar telefono, direccion, voucher ni datos privados.
- Pedir permiso antes de publicar foto de cliente o captura con nombre.
- Si se usa screenshot, tapar apellido, numero y cualquier info sensible.
- Guardar mejores frases en una nota por batch.

Formatos de testimonio:

- Story screenshot + sticker `Review`.
- Carrusel `What people said after the last batch`.
- Reel con texto encima de clips reales de producto.
- Highlight `Reviews` actualizado despues de cada batch.

## Calendario de batch

### 7 dias antes de abrir

- Post feed: anuncio del proximo drop.
- Story: pregunta/poll de sabores.
- CTA: join waitlist.
- Link bio: waitlist.

Copy:

```text
Next Bagelito batch is coming.
Limited packs. Pre-order only.
Join the waitlist through the link in bio.
```

### 5 dias antes

- Reel: behind the scenes o close-up de producto.
- Story: countdown sticker.
- DM/WhatsApp a clientes repetidos.

Copy:

```text
Small batch, made monthly in Lima.
Orders open soon.
```

### 3 dias antes

- Carrusel: packs y precios.
- Story: explain mixed vs single flavor.
- CTA: save this post.

Slides:

1. `Which Bagelito pack should you choose?`
2. `12 mixed`
3. `6 mixed`
4. `12 single flavor`
5. `6 single flavor`
6. `How delivery works`
7. `Orders open soon`

### 1 dia antes

- Story sequence: tomorrow / limited packs / link ready.
- Reel corto: tray visual.
- Update highlight `Next batch`.

Copy:

```text
Orders open tomorrow.
Once packs are gone, the batch closes.
```

### Dia de apertura

- Cambiar bio a version `Orders open now`.
- Cambiar link a `/reserve`.
- Post feed: orders open.
- Stories: link sticker a reserva.
- WhatsApp broadcast suave a waitlist.

Copy:

```text
Orders are open.
Reserve your Bagelito pack for this month's batch.
Limited packs, pre-order only.
```

### Mitad de ventana

- Story: packs left / social proof / FAQ.
- Reel: product detail.
- Responder DMs rapido.

Copy:

```text
Batch window is open.
Pick your pack, choose your flavors, upload Yape/Plin proof.
```

### 24 horas antes de cerrar

- Story: last call.
- Feed optional: last call static or reel.
- WhatsApp a clientes pendientes de pago.

Copy:

```text
Last call for this batch.
Orders close tomorrow or when packs sell out.
```

### Dia de cierre

- Cambiar batch status a `closed` cuando corresponda.
- Story: closing today.
- Post/story: thank you if sold out.
- Cambiar bio a waitlist cuando cierre.

Copy:

```text
Closing today.
If you want Bagelito this month, reserve before the window closes.
```

### Produccion

- Reel: oven / packing / batch table.
- Story: packing list, close-ups, Dawn behind the scenes.
- No mostrar datos de clientes ni direcciones.

Copy:

```text
Batch day.
Every pack is baked, checked and packed before delivery.
```

### Delivery

- Story: out for delivery.
- Story: distrito route without showing private addresses.
- Repost customer photos with permission.

Copy:

```text
Bagelito delivery day.
Packs are on their way across Lima.
```

### Post-delivery

- Story: thank you.
- Ask for feedback/testimonials.
- Add best reviews to `Reviews`.
- Post carousel: batch recap.

Copy:

```text
Thank you for being part of this batch.
Next drop opens soon. Join the waitlist through the link in bio.
```

## Weekly rhythm outside batch

- 1 Reel: product/process.
- 1 Carousel: education, menu or FAQ.
- 2-4 Stories: behind the scenes, polls, reminders.
- 1 Testimonial or customer photo when available.

Keep it sustainable:

- Batch photo/video day should create 2-3 weeks of assets.
- Prioritize original Bagelito footage over trends.
- Reuse the best hooks, but do not repost identical creative too often.

After each post or story push, review:

- Visits to `bagelito.pe` from Instagram UTM.
- Reservations started and completed.
- DMs asking about batch/delivery.
- Waitlist WhatsApp clicks.
